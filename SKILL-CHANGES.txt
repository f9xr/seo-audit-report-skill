CHANGE GROUP 1  ->  SKILL.md v5.2  (YMYL Audit Process)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

1. ADDED: "YMYL Audit Process (Your Money or Your Life)" block
   inside Pillar 16 (E-E-A-T Signals), located after the
   "Content Review Process" bullet. Contains:

   - YMYL Classification Matrix
     Table of 7 classes with detection signals and example topics:
       * Health & Medical
       * Financial
       * Legal
       * News & Current Events
       * Shopping & E-Commerce
       * Education & Career
       * Civic, Government & Safety
     Plus a "Classification rule" (any page whose wrong content could
     affect health, financial stability, safety, or life choices is
     YMYL regardless of intent) and a YMYL page inventory requirement.

   - Per-class hardening standards (each a full bullet):
       * Health & Medical Standards  (credentials/NPI, medical
         review board, PubMed/CDC/WHO citations, disclaimer, 12-month
         freshness)
       * Financial Standards         (CFP/CPA/CFA, IRS/SEC/FINRA
         sources, fee/risk disclosure, no guaranteed-returns)
       * Legal Standards             (attorney + jurisdiction,
         statute/case-law citations, not-legal-advice disclaimer)
       * News & Current Events       (named author, primary-source
         citations, corrections policy, opinion v fact)
       * Shopping & E-Commerce       (review methodology disclosure,
         verified Review schema, moderation, no manipulated pricing)
       * Education & Career          (accreditation/expertise,
         BLS/IPEDS data, affiliate disclosure)
       * Civic, Government & Safety  (official-source citations,
         heightened recency, correction of outdated info)

   - Site Reputation Assessment
     Website-level (not just author-level) reputation as the YMYL
     quality bar, per Google's Aug 2022 "unverified claims" update.

   - Website Owner & Maintainer Transparency
     Named owner/operator, editorial responsibility, funding/ad
     disclosure, update cadence.

   - Harm-Tier Calibration
     Directly harmful / Casually harmful / Informational (support/
     charity) tiers with scaled expertise + source requirements and
     tier-based severity.

6. CORRECTED Pillar 17 (Rich Results Eligibility) - 2023+ deprecations:
   - FAQ Rich Results: deprecated Aug 2023; only highly authoritative
     government / health sites display them. FAQ markup no longer a
     general opportunity (still useful for AI/voice extraction).
   - How-To Rich Results: deprecated on desktop Sept 2023; now only
     mobile long-form how-to VIDEO pages. Not a standalone rich-result
     play.
   - Review Snippet: May 2023 change - only first-party reviews
     (products sold on-site, local businesses) qualify. Flag Review
     schema that cannot meet the first-party bar as wasted markup.

7. ADDED Pillar 2 (Technical SEO) - "Google Spam Policy Compliance
   (March 2024)" sub-block:
   - Site Reputation Abuse (parasite SEO) - sponsored/partner-content
     sections, embedded third-party widgets; flagged High.
   - Scaled Content Abuse - mass-generated template pages with
     near-zero information gain.
   - Expired Domain Abuse - fresh domains claiming old editorial
     history / purchase-driven repurposing; flagged High.
   - Cloaking & Sneaky Redirects - UA/device-based content swaps;
     flagged Critical.
